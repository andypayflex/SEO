A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious?
Good. So are we.

Here’s a little clarity before we say hello.

Can you help with just content or just SEO?+

Absolutely. We can work on a focused copywriting project, a technical audit, or a combined SEO and content plan. We’ll agree on the scope together before getting started.

Who will I be working with?+

You’ll work with professionals whose expertise matches your project, from technical SEO and software engineering to content and copywriting. We coordinate the work around a shared plan, with clear communication throughout.

How long does SEO take to work?+

It depends on your website, competition, and the work involved. Some improvements can be noticed quickly; meaningful changes in organic visibility usually take time. We agree on useful measures at the start and explain progress without promising a particular ranking or deadline.

What does it cost to work with you?+

We quote for the work your business actually needs. After an initial conversation, we’ll share a proposal with deliverables, timing, and pricing so you can make an informed decision.

Do you work with businesses outside Cape Town?+

Yes. We’re set up to collaborate remotely. Wherever you’re based, we can agree on a communication rhythm and working hours that suit the project.
